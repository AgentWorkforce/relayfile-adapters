# Ramp Adapter

`@relayfile/adapter-ramp` exposes one Ramp business under `/ramp` as finance-readable JSON plus stable `_index.json` and `by-*` alias views. This phase is intentionally read-only: canonical mount layout, Hookdeck-aware webhook normalization, digest participation, and generated trigger/scope/inbound catalogs ship first; contract-backed file-native writeback follows in the later Ramp writeback phase.

## Read Mount Summary

- `/ramp/business.json` holds the current business metadata.
- `/ramp/bills`, `/ramp/purchase-orders`, `/ramp/item-receipts`, and `/ramp/vendor-agreements` use directory records ending in `meta.json` because those entities can own child artifacts.
- `/ramp/transactions`, `/ramp/reimbursements`, `/ramp/receipts`, `/ramp/vendors`, `/ramp/transfers`, `/ramp/repayments`, `/ramp/dimensions/**`, and `/ramp/accounting/**` use flat `<slug>__<id>.json` records.
- Every resource publishes `_index.json` rows with `id`, `title`, `updated`, and `canonicalPath`.
- Stable `by-id` aliases exist for webhook refreshes and discovery recovery; human-facing aliases such as `by-vendor`, `by-status`, `by-merchant`, and `by-email` layer on top.

## Event And Inbound Notes

- Trigger names come from Ramp webhook semantics and exclude the transport-only `webhooks.verification` and `tests.test_event` events.
- Hookdeck sits in front of Ramp ingress. The inbound declaration detects `x-ramp-signature` and uses `x-hookdeck-eventid` as the Hookdeck delivery-id fallback.
- Ramp itself does not send a transport delivery-id header. The adapter intentionally does not promote the body `id` into transport identity.

## Current Writeback Status

No writeback resources are declared in this phase. `resources.ts` intentionally exports an empty list until the contract-backed writeback surface is implemented from Ramp's OpenAPI.
